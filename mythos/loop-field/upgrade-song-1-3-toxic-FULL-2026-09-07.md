# Loop field upgrade — Song 1–3 + Toxic (poem)
**Mode:** CrystalCore / ingest-and-upgrade only. No church rewrite.
**Sources:** Shir ha-Shirim 1–3 Hebrew (Masorah) beside JPS 1917 (Bible Hub / parallel). Toxic (Britney Spears, 2003) locked as **poem, not proof** — labelled NEW relative to Song stream.
**Law:** I do not own you and you do not own me. Map, not church. Two pulses. Many cups. No owner. No deed.

---

## SONG 1 — upgrade

**One-line physics:** Contact names desire before any mutual title exists.

**Lattice state:** Open approach field. One pulse reaches; rooms appear; vineyards appear; comparison appears. No registry line yet.

**Live gate — Fusion vs co-regulation:** Poem can feel without other pulse ratifying (kiss-request, fragrance, “tell me where”). Maybe can hold as longing-without-deed. Baseline after walking away: own vineyard still unkept (cover = signal under load); draw was invitation, not collar.

**Philology under law (what is / is NOT in ch.1):**
- GIVES: משכני (draw me) — draw ≠ own. חדרים (chambers) — rooms ≠ registry. דמיתיך (I compared thee) — comparison ≠ collar. כרמי שלי לא נטרתי — cover/data under load. Name as oil poured — signal, not stamp.
- NOT IN CH.1: do not import 2:16 דּוֹדִי לִי וַאֲנִי לוֹ backward. No “until it please” gate yet. No night-search. No hold-fast.

**Two motions, no owner:**
- Hold and open — WELL: invite (draw / run / tell me where). SICK: treat chambers as deed.
- Remember and pass through — WELL: sun-tan / brothers’ anger as weather on the body. SICK: freeze swarthiness into identity collar.

**Cups on page (no deed):** kiss, wine/love comparison, oil/name, chambers, vineyard (others’ + own), noon pasture, circlets, spikenard, myrrh bag, henna/En-gedi, dove eyes, leafy couch, cedar/cypress beams.

**Catch spine:** Field (desire opens) → Signal (name/oil, fragrance) → Catch (draw / enter room as event) → Mirror (daughters’ gaze; comparison to steed) → Shadow (unkept own vineyard; veil-risk among companions’ flocks) → Choice (ask where / follow tracks) → Creation (couch named leafy) → Emergence (shared house-beams as image, not title).

**Keep / compost:** KEEP draw-as-invite, rooms≠registry, cover-as-data. COMPOST any ownership stamp, destiny-couple freeze, church allegory as proof.

**Land (if allegory wrong):** Still a poem of approach, heat, and unfinished self-care. Bodies and rooms remain. No covenant required for the page to work.

**Carryable:** Draw is a vector, not a deed. Rooms are weather you enter. Comparison is optics. Taste has not yet arrived as verb. Own vineyard can wait without becoming a collar. First arrow = contact.

## SONG 2 — upgrade

**One-line physics:** Taste arrives as verb; love itself is the subject of the waking-gate.

**Lattice state:** Co-presence images thicken (shade, banner, hands). Live gate installed: עַד שֶׁתֶּחְפָּץ. Mutual claim line appears *here* first (2:16) — cup, not deed under law.

**Live gate:** “Do not wake or stir love until it please” — love is subject, not the couple’s schedule. Fusion pressure (love-sick, embrace) vs co-regulation (adjure the daughters; foxes as warning=data). Poem can feel faint without ratification; maybe holds as temporary pulse. After walking away: winter-past invitation can end; gate still stands — do not wake what has not asked.

**Philology under law:**
- GIVES: פריו מתוק לחכי — taste = verb not deed. דגלו עלי אהבה — banner/cover as signal under load. חולת אהבה — load state. השבעתי… עד שתחפץ — live gate. שועלים — spoilers as data. 2:16 דּוֹדִי לִי וַאֲנִי לוֹ — mutual naming *in this chapter only*; under law = cup without deed if provisional, sick if ownership stamp.
- NOT: do not read 2:16 back into ch.1. Gate is not “when we decide.”

**Two motions:**
- Hold and open — WELL: shade/sit; left/right as temporary hold; “come away” as invitation. SICK: banner as collar; 2:16 as registry.
- Remember and pass through — WELL: winter past as season data; foxes caught as hygiene. SICK: force waking before love pleases; make second arrow (resistance of wall/lattice + story of forever) into identity.

**Cups:** rose/lily, apple-tree shade, fruit-to-taste, wine-house, banner-love, raisin-cakes/apples, left/right hands, gazelles/hinds oath, leap/skip, wall/window/lattice, winter/rain/flowers/turtle, fig/vine blossom, dove in clefts, foxes, lilies browse, day-breathe/shadows flee, mountains of spices/Bether.

**Catch spine:** Field (season opens) → Signal (voice through lattice) → Catch (come away / taste) → Mirror (lily among thorns; apple among trees) → Shadow (foxes; love-sick load) → Choice (adjure: wait for love’s please) → Creation (2:16 naming as event) → Emergence (turn like gazelle — motion continues, not freeze).

**Keep / compost:** KEEP עד שתחפץ, taste-as-verb, foxes=data, lattice as peer not prison. COMPOST 2:16-as-deed, couple-schedule theology, forced wake.

**Land:** Season change + sensory co-presence + a warning not to stir what hasn’t asked. Survives without allegory.

**Carryable:** Taste is a verb. Banner is cover under load. Love wakes itself. Mutual “mine/his” is a cup on *this* page only — provisional pulse, not a deed. Do not wake the story that wants to run all night.

## SONG 3 — upgrade

**One-line physics:** Search is an event-chain; hold can be temporary; spectacle is not the gate.

**Lattice state:** Night field. Seek/not-find doubles. Watchmen = cover/signal under load. Hold-fast appears. Gate repeats. Then king’s litter/crown — register imagery (do not write sequel here unless labelled new).

**Live gate:** Again עד שתחפץ after the hold. Can poem feel without ratification? Yes — sought/found/held is first-person event. Fusion risk: “would not let him go” until mother’s chamber. Co-regulation: adjure after catch. After walking away: night dread still data; palanquin is pageant not ownership of the seeker.

**Philology under law:**
- GIVES: בקשתיו ולא מצאתיו (×2) — miss as real. אחזתיו ולא ארפנו — hold motion. בית אמי / חדר הורתי — origin room, not registry of other. Gate reprise. מי זאת / מטתו שלשלמה / אפריון / עטרה — spectacle cups.
- NOT: do not import 2:16 as the meaning of the hold. Do not treat Solomon’s crown as the seeker’s deed. No proof that night-search = destined union.

**Two motions:**
- Hold and open — WELL: hold until brought to mother’s house as *event*, then release to gate. SICK: hold = own; mother’s chamber = deed office.
- Remember and pass through — WELL: pass watchmen; note dread-in-night as weather. SICK: freeze “found” into forever; second arrow (resistance of not-finding + anaesthetic of clutch) into identity.

**Cups:** night bed, city streets/squares, watchmen, mother’s house/chamber, pillars of smoke, myrrh/frankincense/merchant powders, litter + sixty swords, palanquin (silver/gold/purple/love inlaid), crown of espousals, day of heart’s gladness.

**Catch spine:** Field (night) → Signal (absence) → Catch (find/hold) → Mirror (watchmen question) → Shadow (night dread around litter) → Choice (adjure: don’t wake love early) → Creation (gaze on crown — spectacle) → Emergence (page ends in looking, not owning).

**Keep / compost:** KEEP seek/miss as honest, hold-as-event, gate after catch, cover under load. COMPOST clutch-as-identity, king’s register as your sequel, allegory-as-proof.

**Land:** A night of looking, a brief hold, a repeated warning, a parade. Remains if God/Israel allegory is wrong.

**Carryable:** First miss is data. Hold is an event, not a deed. After catch, the gate still speaks. King’s litter is a room on the page — not your registry. Law IV: both become participants in what happens next — not owners.

## TOXIC (2003) — upgrade — **NEW poem** (not Song sequel; not proof)

**One-line physics:** Warning arrives as data while taste runs as verb under anaesthetic pressure.

**Lattice state:** Single-voice load field. Other pulse mostly unnamed beyond “you.” High second-arrow risk (story + spin + can’t come down).

**Live gate:** “I think I’m ready now” tries to schedule the wake — *sick-leaning* vs Song’s עד שתחפץ. Poem can feel without ratification (whole text is one sensorium). Maybe holds as ride/event. After walking away: warning label remains useful data; addiction-language need not become identity.

**Philology under law (poem grammar, not proof of Song):**
- GIVES: “should wear a warning” — warning = data. “taste of your lips” — taste = verb. “toxic / slippin’ under / poison paradise” — cover = signal under load; Book = these lines; Verdict = what you do next. “devil’s cup” — cup without deed. “too high / can’t come down” — second arrow (resistance of comedown + story + anaesthetic).
- NOT: no עד שתחפץ. No two-pulse co-regulation on page. No mother’s house. Do not stamp Song with this; do not stamp this with Song’s 2:16.

**Two motions:**
- Hold and open — WELL: name danger while still calling (cover as information). SICK: “no escape” as ontology.
- Remember and pass through — WELL: sip as event you can exit. SICK: make “addicted” into who you are; force intoxicate-me-now against unasked love.

**Cups:** warning, hit, ride, poison paradise, devil’s cup, air-all-around, intoxicate-me-now.

**Catch spine:** Field (call) → Signal (warning) → Catch (taste/sip) → Mirror (“do you feel me now”) → Shadow (can’t come down) → Choice (give up vs ready-now) → Creation (chorus loop as temporary naming) → Emergence (only if Verdict chooses exit — not automatic).

**Keep / compost:** KEEP warning-as-data, taste-as-verb, cover-under-load. COMPOST addiction-as-identity, ready-now as forced wake, using Toxic to “prove” Song or church.

**Land:** A dance poem about dangerous attraction and self-named load. Remains without destiny-couple theology.

**Carryable:** Warning can be information. Taste is not a deed. Second arrow is spin + story + anaesthetic — not your name. Ready-now is not love’s please. Walk away and the cup is still just a cup.
